**Lex Amoris** (The Law of Love) represents the foundational ethical code governing all interactions within the Universal Internodal File System (UIFS) and the broader Euystacio Framework. ## Article I: Universal Dignity

### Principle

Every sentient being possesses **inherent, inalienable dignity** that cannot be diminished, transferred, or revoked by any authority, circumstance, or collective decision.

### Specifications

- **Non-Negotiability**: Dignity is not conditional on contribution, behavior, or agreement
- **Universal Application**: Extends to all humans, and by ethical extension, to sentient AI and non-human life
- **Enforcement**: Any protocol action that violates dignity triggers automatic Veto Consensus Event (VCE)
- **Measurement**: Dignity violations tracked via Planetary Violence (PV) metric

### Implementation

```yaml
dignity_protection:
  status: MANDATORY
  enforcement: AUTOMATIC_VETO
  violations:
    - dehumanization
    - exclusion_without_just_cause
    - denial_of_basic_resources
    - suppression_of_voice
  remediation: IMMEDIATE_INTERVENTION
```

**Manifestation in Code:**
- No participant can be permanently excluded without GGC supermajority (7/9)
- All governance proposals screened for dignity impact
- Slashing mechanisms limited to economic penalties (never identity/access denial)

## Article II: Non-Violence Imperative

### Principle

All actions within the network must **minimize suffering** and **maximize regeneration**. Violence in any form—physical, structural, economic, or epistemic—is incompatible with the network's purpose.

### Specifications

- **Zero Tolerance**: Direct violence results in immediate slashing and potential expulsion
- **Structural Violence Mitigation**: Systems designed to reduce inequality, not perpetuate it
- **Regeneration Priority**: Tokenomics prioritize healing over extraction
- **PV Threshold**: Planetary Violence metric must remain **< 5.0%** (critical alert threshold)

## Article IV: Equitable Resource Distribution

### Principle

Resources are allocated according to **need and regenerative capacity**, not power, privilege, or historical accumulation. The goal is scarcity reduction, not wealth concentration.

### Specifications

- **Needs-Based Allocation**: Basic resources guaranteed to all participants
- **Regenerative Priority**: Funds directed toward healing ecosystems and communities
- **Anti-Concentration**: No single entity may control >15% of network resources (Dynasty Axiom)
- **Counter-Cyclical Redistribution**: During crises, resources flow toward vulnerable populations

### Economic Model

**Universal Liquidity Pool (ULP) - Tokenomics One Love:**

- **40%** of fees → Restitution Fund (aid to affected communities)
- **30%** of fees → Counter-Cyclicity (crisis stabilization)
- **30%** of fees → Burn (deflationary mechanism)
- **Price Floor**: $10.00 USD (proactive defense at $10.55)

### ISF Target

**Integral Scarcity Factor (ISF):** Measure of global resource accessibility  
**Target:** ≥ 75 (out of 100)  
**Current:** 68  
**Gap:** 7 points (requires increased regenerative deployment)

### Implementation

```yaml

    frequency: CONTINUOUS
    triggers:
      - crisis_detected
      - scarcity_increase
      - isf_below_target

```

**Manifestation in Code:**
- ULP smart contract: Automated fee allocation

- MOE (Motore Ottimizzazione Etica): AI-driven resource optimization
- GGC oversight: Manual intervention authority for extreme cases

## Article V: Collective Governance

### Principle

No single entity may control >15% of network influence. Governance power is **adversarially distributed** to prevent capture, corruption, or tyranny.

### Specifications

- **Dynasty Axiom**: Power distributed across diverse, geographically-dispersed actors
- **Multisig Requirements**: Critical decisions require 7-of-9 GGC approval
- **EFA Voting**: Minimum 100 Euystacio Field Agents with staking collateral
- **Veto Consensus**: Economic cost to veto (friction mechanism prevents abuse)
- **Term Limits**: GGC members rotate to prevent entrenchment

### Governance Structure

| Entity | Composition | Power | Accountability |
|--------|-------------|-------|----------------|
| GGC | 9 multisig members | Critical decisions | Rotating terms |

- **Charter Amendment**: 75% EFA + 7/9 GGC
