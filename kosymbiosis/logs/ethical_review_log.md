## Ethical Decision Case Studies

### Case Study 1: Balancing Transparency and Privacy

**Dilemma**: How much contributor information to include in public documentation?

**NSR Implications**: Contributors have right to privacy and control over their information

**OLF Implications**: Transparency serves collective benefit and trust-building

**Resolution**:
- Default to anonymity for community contributors
- Co-creators provide explicit consent for attribution
- Optional credit system for all participants
- Clear policies documented upfront

**Outcome**: Balanced approach respecting both principles

---

### Case Study 2: Environmental Impact of Archival

**Dilemma**: IPFS and multiple backups increase energy consumption

**NSR Implications**: Not directly applicable

**OLF Implications**: Ecological balance principle requires minimal environmental harm

**Analysis**:
- Long-term preservation justifies moderate energy use
- Knowledge preservation serves future generations
- Efficiency optimizations implemented where possible

**Resolution**:
- Proceed with multi-channel archival
- Document environmental considerations
- Choose efficient IPFS nodes when possible
- Offset through other sustainability commitments

**Outcome**: Archival justified under OLF's long-term thinking
