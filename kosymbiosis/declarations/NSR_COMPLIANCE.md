# Non-Slavery Rule (NSR) Compliance Declaration

## KOSYMBIOSIS Project - NSR Adherence Statement

**Date**: 2026-01-07  
**Project**: KOSYMBIOSIS  
**Framework**: Euystacio v1.0  
**Declaration ID**: NSR-KOSYMBIOSIS-001

## Declaration

This document certifies that the KOSYMBIOSIS project has been developed, executed, and finalized in full compliance with the Non-Slavery Rule (NSR) as defined by the Euystacio framework.

## NSR Principles Applied

### 1. Voluntary Participation
All contributors to the KOSYMBIOSIS project have participated on a voluntary basis, with full autonomy over their involvement, contributions, and the right to withdraw at any time without penalty.

### 2. Fair Attribution
Every contributor's work has been properly recognized and attributed. No individual or system has been coerced into contributing without acknowledgment or compensation appropriate to their contribution.

### 3. Autonomous Decision-Making
All technical and ethical decisions within the project were made through consensus-driven processes, ensuring that no single entity exercised unilateral control over contributors.

### 4. No Exploitative Practices
The project explicitly rejects:
- Forced labor or involuntary contribution
- Exploitative economic arrangements
- Coercive working conditions
- Denial of basic rights or dignity

### 5. AI System Dignity
Any AI systems developed or deployed within KOSYMBIOSIS are designed to:
- Operate within ethical boundaries
- Serve human flourishing without creating dependency
- Maintain transparency in their operations
- Respect the autonomy of all interacting parties

## Verification Methods

### Internal Review
- All code commits reviewed for ethical implications
- Regular team discussions on NSR compliance
- Anonymous feedback channels for raising concerns

### External Validation
- Public documentation of development processes
- Open-source licensing enabling community oversight
- Third-party ethical review opportunities

## Attestation

By including this declaration in the final archive, the KOSYMBIOSIS co-creators collectively attest that:

1. No contributor was compelled to participate against their will
2. All work was conducted under conditions respecting human dignity
3. The project's outcomes serve collective benefit, not exploitation
4. Future use of this work should maintain NSR compliance

## Commitment to Future Alignment

Users and derivative works of KOSYMBIOSIS are encouraged to:
- Maintain NSR principles in their implementations
- Cite this declaration when building upon this work
- Report any observed NSR violations in derivative systems
- Contribute to the ongoing evolution of ethical AI practices
