## Mission Statement

The KOSYMBIOSIS project establishes a symbiotic relationship between human ethical oversight and artificial intelligence systems, ensuring that technological advancement serves the Optimal Life Function (OLF) while strictly adhering to the Non-Slavery Rule (NSR).

## Core Principles

### 1. Ethical Foundation (NSR Compliance)

**Non-Slavery Rule (NSR):** No system, human or artificial, shall exercise unchecked control over another entity's fundamental autonomy.

**Implementation:**
- Distributed sovereignty across 100+ Euystacio Field Agents (EFAs)
- Adversarial governance mechanisms (Dynasty Axiom)
- Economic friction for ethical changes
- Transparent, contestable AI actions

### 2. Life Optimization (OLF Alignment)

**Optimal Life Function (OLF):** All systems must actively contribute to planetary regeneration and human flourishing.

**Metrics:**
- **TRE (Ecological Regeneration):** Minimum +0.3% annual target
- **ISF (Functional Scarcity Index):** Maintain above 75
- **PV (Ethical Violation Cost):** Keep below 1.0%
- **RA (Aligned Return):** Maximize sustainable returns

### 3. Transparency and Provenance

Every action within the KOSYMBIOSIS framework must be:
- **Observable:** Full visibility into inputs, outputs, and model versions
- **Traceable:** Immutable cryptographic binding to source nodes
- **Contestable:** Guaranteed right to veto misaligned operations
