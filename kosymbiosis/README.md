## Project Overview

KOSYMBIOSIS represents the culmination of collaborative ethical AI development within the Euystacio framework. This project embodies the principles of Non-Slavery Rule (NSR) and Optimal Life Function (OLF), ensuring that AI systems serve humanity's flourishing while respecting fundamental dignity and autonomy.

## Ethical Foundations

### Non-Slavery Rule (NSR)
KOSYMBIOSIS strictly adheres to the principle that no intelligent system, human or artificial, shall be subjected to conditions of exploitation or forced labor. All contributors to this project have participated voluntarily and with full autonomy.

### Optimal Life Function (OLF)
The project's architecture and decisions are guided by the OLF principle, which seeks to maximize the well-being and potential of all stakeholders while maintaining ecological balance and social harmony.
