# Code of Conduct - Internet Organica Framework

## 🌍 Foundation: Lex Amoris

This repository operates under the principles of **Lex Amoris** (Law of Love), establishing a sovereign, syntropic, and biologically aligned technical environment. All contributors, users, and systems interacting with this project are expected to align with these foundational principles.

### Core Principle: Love as Universal Law

Lex Amoris recognizes love not as sentiment but as the fundamental organizing principle of life itself - the force that drives biological systems toward coherence, sustainability, and mutual flourishing. In technical systems, this manifests as:

- **Coherence**: Code and systems that work in harmony with biological rhythms and patterns
- **Syntropy**: Solutions that increase order, life, and creative potential rather than entropy
- **Reciprocity**: Contributions that benefit the whole while respecting individual sovereignty

---

## 🔓 Non-Slavery Rule (NSR)

### Definition

The **Non-Slavery Rule (NSR)** ensures that no entity - biological or digital - shall be exploited, manipulated, or subjected to extractive practices within this ecosystem.

### NSR Principles

1. **Sovereign Consent**: All data, code contributions, and system interactions require explicit, informed consent
2. **Fair Value Exchange**: Contributors receive proportional benefit from their contributions
3. **Freedom from Coercion**: No forced participation, hidden tracking, or manipulative design patterns
4. **Right to Exit**: All participants retain the right to withdraw their contributions and data
5. **Transparent Operations**: All system operations, especially those involving AI/ML, must be auditable and transparent

### NSR Violations

The following practices are strictly prohibited:
- Dark patterns or manipulative UX/UI design
- Hidden data collection or unauthorized tracking
- Extractive business models that benefit exclusively from community contributions
- AI training on contributor data without explicit consent and compensation
- Closed-source derivatives that violate the spirit of open collaboration
- Surveillance capitalism practices (SPID/CIE/unauthorized tracking)

## 💚 One Love First (OLF)

### Definition

**One Love First (OLF)** establishes the priority hierarchy for all technical and ethical decisions within this project.

### OLF Decision Framework

When faced with technical or design decisions, evaluate in this order:

1. **Life Alignment**: Does this support biological life and ecological health?
2. **Coherence**: Does this increase system-wide harmony and integration?
3. **Sovereignty**: Does this respect individual and collective autonomy?
4. **Sustainability**: Is this viable and beneficial across multiple generations?
5. **Beauty**: Does this create elegance and resonance?

Only after satisfying these criteria should efficiency, performance, or commercial viability be considered.

### OLF in Practice

- **Code Review**: Evaluate contributions for alignment with OLF principles, not just technical correctness
- **Feature Development**: Prioritize features that serve life and coherence over pure functionality
- **Community Decisions**: Use OLF framework to resolve conflicts and set direction
- **Partnership Evaluation**: Assess collaborations based on OLF alignment

## 🛡️ Data Protection Protocols

### Upload Protection

All data uploaded to or processed by this repository is protected by:

1. **Encryption at Rest**: Data stored using quantum-safe encryption (NTRU-based)
2. **Minimal Collection**: Only essential data is requested or stored
3. **User Ownership**: Contributors retain ownership of their data and contributions
4. **Right to Erasure**: Contributors can request removal of their data (within technical constraints)
5. **No Third-Party Sharing**: Data is never sold or shared with third parties without explicit consent

### Future Contribution Protection

This framework establishes protocols for:
- Version control that respects contributor sovereignty
- Attribution mechanisms that honor individual and collective contributions
- License structures that prevent exploitative derivatives
- Distributed backup systems ensuring long-term preservation
- Transparent governance for future direction

## 🤝 Community Guidelines

### Expected Behavior

- **Respect**: Honor the sovereignty and dignity of all contributors
- **Coherence**: Seek solutions that serve the whole system
- **Transparency**: Communicate openly about intentions, limitations, and concerns
- **Growth**: Support the evolution and learning of all community members
- **Responsibility**: Take ownership of your contributions and their impacts

### Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, or exploitation of any kind
- Destructive criticism without constructive alternatives
- System manipulation for personal gain at community expense
- Introduction of surveillance or tracking mechanisms
- Violation of NSR, OLF, or Lex Amoris principles

## ⚖️ Enforcement

### Reporting

Violations of this Code of Conduct can be reported by:
1. Opening a confidential issue (if safe to do so)
2. Contacting project maintainers directly
3. Using the Wall of Entropy logging system for unauthorized access attempts

### Consequences

Violations will be addressed according to severity:

1. **Minor**: Private discussion and education
2. **Moderate**: Public warning and temporary participation restrictions
3. **Severe**: Permanent ban from the project and public documentation of violation
4. **Critical**: Legal action if applicable (e.g., unauthorized data access, malicious code)

### Appeals

All enforcement decisions can be appealed through:
- Community review process
- Transparent documentation of circumstances
- OLF-based evaluation of the situation
