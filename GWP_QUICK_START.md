- **No one can alter** the foundational documents
- **Everyone can verify** authenticity by comparing CID hashes

## ❓ Common Questions

### Q: Is this a cryptocurrency project?
A: The UIFS uses tokenomics (Universal Liquidity Pool with $SAIN token), but the primary purpose is **ethical AI governance and regenerative resource distribution**, not financial speculation.

### Q: Do I need technical skills to participate?
A: No! You can participate by:
- Reading and sharing the philosophy
- Providing feedback on governance proposals
- Translating documents
- Joining community discussions

Technical contributions (running nodes, developing code) are welcome but optional.

### Q: Who controls the Global Welcome Protocol?
A: The GWP is governed by the **Global Governance Council (GGC)** using a 7-of-9 multisig system, combined with **Euystacio Field Agents (EFA)** voting. No single entity has unilateral control.

### Q: Can the foundational documents be changed?
A: **Lex Amoris Articles I-III** (Dignity, Non-Violence, Transparency) are **immutable** and cannot be amended. Articles IV-VI can be amended only through a 75% EFA vote + 7-of-9 GGC approval.

### Q: How do I verify the content is authentic?
A: Every document has an IPFS CID that you can verify:
1. Download the document from IPFS
2. Compute its hash: `ipfs add --only-hash filename`
3. Compare with the on-chain CID in the TFKVerifier contract

### Q: Is my personal data collected?
A: **No.** The GWP does not collect personal information. Only wallet addresses/DIDs are recorded on-chain for governance purposes. All content is public by design.
